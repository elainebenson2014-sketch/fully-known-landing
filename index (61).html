The Fully Known journey

Healing begins with being seen.

Through biblical teaching, guided reflection, prayer and practical tools, Fully Known helps people examine identity, beliefs, emotional patterns and the freedom that comes through surrender.

01

Know your identity

Replace labels, rejection and false agreements with what God says is true.

02

Understand your story

Recognize patterns, emotions and responses with honesty, compassion and wisdom.

03

Walk in freedom

Practice new ways of living rooted in Christ, healthy boundaries and purpose.

Fully Known Foundations certificate program

Eight weeks. A deeper understanding of who you are.

Created and facilitated by Dr. Elaine Benson, this Bible-based program helps participants recognize how past experiences and deeply held beliefs influence their lives — and grow in the assurance that they are fully known and deeply loved by God.

What you will explore

  • Identity in Christ and the beliefs that shape us
  • False agreements, rejection and fear
  • Belonging and freedom from performance-based worth
  • Forgiveness, relationships and healthy boundaries
  • Prayer, surrender and practical steps toward spiritual growth

Before you begin

Common questions

How long is the certificate program?

Fully Known Foundations is an eight-week program with weekly Sunday sessions, October 25 through December 13, 2026.

How much does the program cost?

Program tuition is $199. Registration is completed through the learning portal.

How do I register?

Click any “Register for Foundations” button to visit the learning portal, create your account, and enroll.

How do I receive my certificate?

Participants who complete the eight-week Foundations coursework receive a certificate of completion through the learning portal.

Are there resources for children and teens?

Yes — Fully Known Kids (ages 5–12) and Fully Known Teens (ages 13–17) are coming soon. Reach out to be notified when classes open.

Can I schedule an individual session beyond the class?

Yes. Individual pastoral care and prayer appointments are available. Start with a free 15-minute initial conversation.

Does Fully Known replace counseling or medical care?

No. Sessions provide Christian spiritual support and do not replace medical or licensed mental health care.

There is room for your story.

Register for Foundations starting October 25, 2026. Part 1, Part 2, Kids and Teens classes are coming soon. Request an individual pastoral care session, or ask about books, ministry groups and merchandise.
